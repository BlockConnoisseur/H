# Halo Forge
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
People launching funded Zcash cryptography research agents; researchers inspecting evidence; independent reviewers judging contributions.

## Product Purpose
Help agent deployers fund bounded experiments and inspect real, independently verified improvements to Zcash proving performance.

## Operating Context
The user confirmed research progress first, with token details on each agent. Use the supplied research pack at ../halo-forge-research as product evidence. Each agent has a token paired with the approved Solana ZEC representation. Awards automatically go to its immutable original deployer after review and the dispute period.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Build overview, agent directory/details, launch, challenges/details, findings/details, leaderboard, rewards, compute budgets, reviewer workspace and settings. Maintain persistent accounting and session ownership. No real discoveries, funded prizes or deployed contracts exist yet. Preview examples must be labeled. Mainnet launch, final independent evaluation and payouts require configured, verified integrations. The requested 3% total fee and 1% shared compute allocation remain unresolved at the full route level; do not silently allocate the remainder.

## Brand Commitments
Working name Halo Forge. Only shadcn/ui components and pbakaus/impeccable design guidance. No other UI kits, stock templates or image-generation design sources. Direct language and visible evidence. The user selected Hashsmashers as a structural reference, then explicitly required clean non-pixel typography, predominantly black and white surfaces, and subtle gold accents. This supersedes the earlier green identity.

## Evidence on Hand
Pinned upstream sources, actual older Orchard smoke run, draft competition rules, example 10 ZEC budget with zero real funding. No measured AI speedup.

## Product Principles
Research evidence precedes financial promotion. Preserve deployer identity. Bound compute before spending. Make pending, reviewed and paid states unambiguous.

## Built-in research team

User requirement: include three platform agents from initial setup, each using a different improvement method. Bucket Scout investigates scalar reuse, Scratch Worker investigates invocation-local memory reuse, and Thread Weaver investigates public-size scheduling. New agents default to balanced research coverage and receive an unoccupied method/workload assignment. Sessions preserve that assignment. Execution remains inactive until the worker and funding infrastructure is connected.

## Identity refinement

User rejected the coin-style logo and flat presentation. Use the original three-part Halo Forge mark, a light interactive method diagram panel, clearer method illustrations and stronger type hierarchy. Preserve black/white dominance, quiet gold and no pixel fonts.
